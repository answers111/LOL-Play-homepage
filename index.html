Abstract

Visual agents must coordinate reasoning and action when the environment continues to evolve during inference. We introduce LOLPlay, a real-time visual-agent benchmark in League of Legends. Its 36 champion-specific tasks combine resource management, combat, cooperation, and objectives through screenshots and mouse–keyboard actions. Each task includes automated initialization and executable state/event evaluation, with 82 subgoal checks distinguishing partial progress from completion. Across 324 task evaluations of three models at three reasoning-effort settings, the highest task success rate is 25.0%, shared by GPT-6 Astra at low and medium effort; medium effort also achieves the highest subgoal success rate of 40.3%. High effort improves neither metric over low effort for any model. Analyses reveal incomplete subgoal composition and actions executing after the observed scene changes, motivating agents that integrate visual reasoning with timely control.

Executable Evaluation

Task completion is verified independently from the agent's final message. A successful task satisfies every required condition, including its event order and deadline.

EvidenceWhat is checkedExamples
Final statePlayer state after terminationLevel, inventory, skill ranks, attribute growth
Deadline stateA snapshot at the prescribed endpointHealth, mana, survival at the deadline
Recorded eventsEvent identity, timestamp, and required orderPersonal kills, assists, dragons, and turrets

Challenge Phenomena

Familiar desktop inputs must be coordinated with a changing world. The benchmark exposes several recurring demands on real-time visual agents.

PhenomenonControl demand
Observation–action delayReasoning continues while the scene evolves.Refresh observations and replan when enemies, combat, or interfaces change.
Subgoal compositionPartial progress does not guarantee completion.Satisfy all resource, skill, survival, and objective conditions together.
Deadline controlSome outcomes must hold at a specific endpoint.Maintain the required state until the task's time budget expires.
Ordered objectivesCorrect outcomes can require a particular sequence.Personally kill a Voidgrub before destroying the target turret.
Visual groundingThe agent receives no structured unit-state feed.Read the HUD, localize targets, allocate abilities, and navigate the shop from pixels.

Human feasibility check. Two players, each with more than 200 prior League of Legends matches, cross-validated the full suite under the same configurations, budgets, and evaluator. All 36 tasks were completed. This verifies task feasibility; it is not a population-level estimate of human performance.
